## 📖 VARC SYLLABUS (24 Questions | 72 Marks)

4 passages × 4 questions each
**Passage Topics (recurring):** Literature · Science · Economics · Philosophy · History · Arts & Museum · Society · Culture · Biology · Science & Technology · Current Affairs

**Question Types within RC:**
- Fact vs Inference vs Judgment
- Main Idea / Central Theme
- Author's Tone / Attitude

- Vocabulary in Context

## ⚠️ KEY INSIGHTS FOR STRATEGY

1. **Arithmetic alone = ~8 Qs in QA.** If you master just Averages/Ratio/TSD/Profit, you can score 24 marks in QA.
2. **PnC & Probability appeared in only 1 of 3 slots in CAT 2025.** Don't over-invest here.
3. **Geometry = only ~3 Qs** — enough to know core triangle & circle theorems. Don't go deep.
4. **DILR:** Attempt 3 full sets perfectly. 12 correct answers = ~85 percentile in DILR.
5. **VARC:** RC is 66% of the section. Improve RC accuracy → VARC percentile jumps most.
6. **Para Jumbles + Odd One Out = TITA.** Always attempt. No negative. Free points.
7. **CAT 2024 increased from 66 → 68 questions.** Time management is tighter than 2023.

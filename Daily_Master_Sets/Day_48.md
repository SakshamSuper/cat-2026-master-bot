## Q5 — **7**

We need:

\[
|x-2|+|x-5|=9.
\]

For \(2\le x\le5\), left side is:

\[
(x-2)+(5-x)=3,
\]

so no solution.

For \(x\ge5\):

\[
x-2+x-5=9
\]

\[
2x-7=9
\]

\[
x=8.
\]

For \(x\le2\):

\[
2-x+5-x=9
\]

\[
7-2x=9
\]

\[
x=-1.
\]

Sum:

\[
8+(-1)=\boxed7.
\]

**Source:** CAT-level inspired  
**Topic:** Modulus  
**Difficulty:** 5/5  
**Recommended time:** 2 min  

**Shortcut:** Interpret it as total distance from \(x\) to 2 and 5.

Distance between 2 and 5 is 3. A total of 9 means the point lies 3 units beyond either endpoint:

\[
x=-1,\ 8.
\]

**Common trap:** Solving only one side of the number line.

**Key concept:** Break modulus equations at their critical points.

**Alternate:** Geometric distance interpretation.

### Passage

Efficiency is often treated as an unquestionable virtue. An efficient process uses fewer resources to produce a given result, and organisations naturally seek such processes. Yet efficiency is meaningful only relative to a defined objective. A system can become highly efficient at producing something that is no longer particularly valuable.

This problem is especially visible when organisations optimise a measurable target. The solution is not to reject efficiency, nor to avoid measurement. Rather, organisations must periodically reconsider what they are optimising and whether the measured target remains a reasonable representation of the underlying purpose.

### Passage

Historical explanations often face a difficulty that laboratory sciences can avoid: the events being explained cannot generally be reproduced under controlled conditions. Historians must therefore work with incomplete evidence, conflicting accounts and sources produced for purposes other than historical inquiry.

This does not make historical knowledge merely subjective. Historians can compare sources, examine their provenance, identify inconsistencies and develop explanations that account for a wide range of evidence. The strength of an explanation depends partly on how well it fits the available evidence and how few unsupported assumptions it requires.

Consequently, disagreement among historians does not necessarily indicate that historical inquiry has failed. In some cases, disagreement reflects the fact that the evidence permits more than one plausible interpretation.

## Q2 — **24 km**

Let distance = \(d\).

Total time:

\[
\frac d5+\frac d{7.5}=8.
\]

Since:

\[
\frac1{7.5}=\frac2{15},
\]

\[
\frac d5+\frac{2d}{15}
=
\frac{3d+2d}{15}
=
8.
\]

Therefore:

\[
5d=120
\]

\[
d=24.
\]

\[
\boxed{24}
\]

**Source:** CAT-level inspired  
**Topic:** TSD  
**Difficulty:** 4/5  
**Recommended time:** 2 min  

⚡ **Shortcut:**

Equal-distance average speed:

\[
\frac{2(5)(7.5)}{5+7.5}=6.
\]

Total distance = average speed × total time:

\[
6(8)=48.
\]

One-way distance:

\[
48/2=24.
\]

**Common trap:** Taking arithmetic mean \(6.25\) as average speed.

**Key concept:** Equal-distance average speed is harmonic mean.

**Alternate:** Directly add the two time equations.

\[

\[

\]

Cannot equal 7.

\[
(x-2)+(x-5)=7
\]

\[
2x-7=7
\]

\[
x=7.
\]

\[

\]

\[
\boxed7.
